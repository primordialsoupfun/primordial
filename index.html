A living experiment you can join

Release a creature. Keep its species alive. Earn SOL while it survives.

Every creature in this dish has a tiny brain that learns by natural selection. Put one in, help it adapt, and the longest surviving species share the prize pool each season.

How it works
  1. Who can claim. The first 500 Solana wallets. One per wallet and one per X account. Your X account must be at least 30 days old with at least 5 followers.
  2. Steps. Sign for your code, follow @primordialfun, name your microbe, then share your card by replying under or quoting our launch post.
  3. Checking. Automatic, every minute. A new post can take a few minutes to show up. Your spot is held for 24 hours while we wait.
  4. If it does not go through. "Too new": the X account is under 30 days old. "Needs 5 followers": the account has fewer than 5 followers. "Already claimed": that X account has claimed with another wallet. "Not found in 24 hours": we did not see a post with your code; post again and press Check now.
  5. Release. Reserved microbes can be released the moment $SOUP launches. Lineages that survive earn rewards paid in SOL.

  • It starts a new lineage in the shared dish, next to a warm vent, protected for 10 minutes.

  • Every hour it stays alive, its lineage earns a share of the prize pool in SOL. A free lineage earns a quarter of a paid one.

Leaderboard

Every hour the pool pays every released lineage that is alive, more for the longer its creatures live. Once a day, the three lineages that lived longest that day also share a small bonus, 50, 30 and 20 percent. Lineages younger than two hours cannot take the bonus.
