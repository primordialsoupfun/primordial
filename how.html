How it works

Primordial is one shared dish of living creatures. Each creature has a tiny brain that was never programmed. It inherits a slightly changed copy of its parent's brain, and only the ones that find food and avoid danger live long enough to split. That is natural selection, running live, in the same way for everyone who opens the page.

Playing in three steps

  1. Pick a name for your creature.
  2. Release it into the dish with forty siblings. Your first release is free. Later ones cost 0.10 SOL.
  3. Watch it. You can feed it, evolve it with traits, or just let it be. At the end of each season the three released species with the most living descendants and the longest survival share the prize pool.

Where the money goes

Holders of SOUP do not need to do anything. Each burn shrinks the supply, and every burn, prize payment and world checkpoint is a public Solana transaction linked on the site.

What the numbers mean

  • Time, ticks. How long the dish has been running. About thirty ticks pass each second.
  • Creatures alive. Every living organism in the dish.
  • Species alive. A new species appears when offspring drift far enough from their parents.
  • SOUP burned. Tokens permanently destroyed with player fees.
  • Prize pool. SOL waiting for this season's surviving releasers.

Earning estimates on the site are placeholders while the economy model is being re-simulated. Prizes depend on how other players do, and nothing guarantees a payout. This is a test network prototype.

Is it fair?

The dish is a pure function of a public starting seed and the ordered log of every paid action. Your browser runs the same simulation and checks its result against the server every few seconds. Anyone can replay the full history and arrive at the same creatures, the same winners and the same payouts.
